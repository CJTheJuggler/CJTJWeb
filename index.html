Home Page

Hello there, I'm CJ; the performer behined CJ The Juggler.

I've been clowning about for over 10 years and specialise in Juggling and poi. Both of which are skills I'd love to share with you if i get the chance to.

As a performer I love to make an atmosphere where anyone can enjoy any of my performances, Not only that i strive to make sure anyone no matter their ability can try out circus skills though adapting the skills to best fit their need.

My main aim is to welcome you to this verry inclusive and diverse comunity of Circus. Contact me through my website (or social media) if you have any questions or would like a quote on a performance or workshop! I hope to be hearing from you soon.
